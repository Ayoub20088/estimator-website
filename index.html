Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. There's no sign-up and no password.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone or iPad and in your own private iCloud. We have no server and never see your estimates, invoices or clients.

What happens when my subscription ends?

Everything you made stays in the app and you can always open it. To change or send those documents again, or to make new ones, subscribe again. Your 3 free estimates stay yours.

Can I use it on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Sign in to the same Apple Account and your work syncs through iCloud.

Does voice need internet?

Yes. Speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition. The rest of the app works without internet.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Estimator. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

I changed phones. How do I get Pro back?

Open the plans screen and tap “Already subscribed? Restore purchase”, signed in with the same Apple Account.
